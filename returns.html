Returns & replacements.

We want you to feel looked after if something arrives damaged or defective. This is a draft policy and must be confirmed before launch.

Damaged or defective items

Please contact us within 7 days for damaged or defective items (default draft; confirm the final return window) of delivery. Include your order number, a description of the issue, and clear photos where possible so we can review your request.

Review and resolution

We will review the information and let you know whether a replacement, repair, or refund is available. Do not send an item back before we provide return instructions.

Refunds

If a refund is approved, it will be returned through the original payment method where possible. The time for the credit to appear may depend on your bank or payment provider.
