YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy.

This draft explains what customer information Needverse uses to provide accounts, fulfil orders, and support customers.

Information we collect

When you create an account or place an order, we may collect your name, email address, delivery address, and order history. We use this information to manage your account, process and fulfil orders, respond to support requests, and meet applicable record-keeping requirements.

Service providers

We use Supabase for customer accounts and order data, Razorpay to process payments, and Resend to send order and account emails. These providers process information as needed to provide their services to Needverse.

Needverse does not sell customer personal information.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed to operate the store, provide support, and meet legal obligations. We use service providers and access controls intended to protect customer information.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can request access to or deletion of your account information by emailing . Some order records may need to be kept where applicable law requires it.
